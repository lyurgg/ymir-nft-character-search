The item code is the fourth data word in the marketplace’s purchase event. On Scan, open Event Logs and change that word from Hex to UTF-8 to read the code. English names come from the item dictionary; the receipt itself records the code.

A stopped or failed scan is labelled incomplete. CSV exports retain that status. Amounts are the gross purchase price, before seller fees. A code identifies an item type; it does not identify a unique item instance or establish its enhancement level.
